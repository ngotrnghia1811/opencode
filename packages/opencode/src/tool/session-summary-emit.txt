Emit a session-summary YAML artifact for an @aki-agents long-running session at its stop ritual.

Persists turn-by-turn arc (high-level summaries only, no raw message bodies), aggregate stats, open threads, and the redaction policy applied at write time to `.opencode/aki-agents/session-<ts>.yaml`.

Redaction policy options:
- `none`: write as-is
- `denylist`: scrub common secret patterns (sk-*, ghp_*, AKIA*, openai-/openrouter-/anthropic- key prefixes, long opaque tokens) in text fields
- `strict`: also scrub lines that loosely resemble credentials (key/secret/token/password followed by separator)

Only @aki-agents has permission to fire this tool. Per Principle 7 (trainability), this artifact is the only per-session record available for the session-tier scorer in F14.
